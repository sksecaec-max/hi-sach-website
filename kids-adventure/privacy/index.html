Kids Adventure — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 29 October 2026
Publisher: Hi-Sach
Contact: hi.sach2027@gmail.com

Kids Adventure is a learning and play app for children aged about 2 to 10. We built it so that it does not need to know anything about your child, and it does not send anything about your child anywhere.

The short version

  • Kids Adventure works fully offline.
  • It has no ads, no accounts, no sign-in, no in-app purchases, and no tracking or analytics services.
  • It does not collect, send, sell or share any personal information. We, the publisher, never receive any data from the app.
  • What the app remembers (described below) stays on your device, and you can delete it at any time.

What the app keeps on your device

To work, the app saves a small amount of information in the device's own app storage:

WhatWhy
The child's first name or nickname, if you type one (the default is "friend")So Momo can greet your child
The age group you choose (for example 5–6) and a picture avatarTo pick suitable activities
Stars, badges, stickers, which activities were played and how they went, a play streak, and simple play-time totalsTo show progress in the Parent Zone and to adjust difficulty
Settings (sound, music, motion, text size, contrast, voice)To remember your choices

This information never leaves the device through the app. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

Device backups. If Android's own backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's storage in your device backup to your Google account, like it does for other apps. That backup is controlled by you and Google; we have no access to it.

Sharing an achievement picture

In the Parent Zone, a grown-up can choose to share a picture of an achievement. Sharing is behind a parent check. The picture shows no name and nothing personal. It is handed to your device's own share menu, and you choose where it goes; the app sends it nowhere by itself.

Permissions

The app does not ask for access to the camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. The Android package lists the standard Internet permission that its app framework includes by default; the app does not use it to send or receive any data.

Voices and sounds

All voices and sounds are inside the app. Speech is played from recordings made in advance; if a line has no recording, the app asks the device's own text-to-speech engine (chosen in your Android settings) to read it. Nothing is recorded from your child.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children. We designed it to meet the expectations of children's privacy rules such as the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the EU/UK GDPR and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and Google Play's Families Policy.

Deleting the information on your device

  • Parent Zone → Settings → Reset all progress clears progress, stars and badges (you can keep or change the child's profile there too).
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on the device.
  • You can also clear it with Android Settings → Apps → Kids Adventure → Storage → Clear storage.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app. If a future version ever needed to collect any information, it would ask for a parent's permission first.
